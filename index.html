Privacy Policy

What we collect

To run the app, we store your email address, your week completion progress, and your Spirit Within daily tags (e.g. "Empowered," "Comforted"). That's it.

What we don't collect

Your written reflections, your conversation responses, and anything you type into the app stays on your device only. It is never sent to our servers, never stored, and never seen by anyone — including the author.

Your data is yours

You can delete your account at any time. Deleting your account permanently removes all stored data.

No third-party sharing

We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to any third party.
